Every business that uses WhatsApp ends up answering the same messages again and again: *What are your timings? How much does it cost? Can I book for Saturday?* Today we're making it much easier to hand those conversations to a bot, without losing the human touch.

**Flows** is the place in BigBrosAI where you build WhatsApp chatbots without writing a single line of code. You can build visually, block by block, or simply describe your business and let AI build the whole thing for you.

## What you can build with Flows

A flow is a conversation map. It starts when a customer sends a message, and then guides them with buttons, questions and answers until they get what they need, or reach a real person.

Businesses on BigBrosAI use flows to:

- **Book appointments** for clinics, salons and service businesses
- **Qualify leads** from ads before a salesperson calls
- **Answer FAQs** about timings, prices, delivery and returns, 24/7
- **Take orders** and send payment links inside the chat
- **Route chats** to the right team member with full context

![A dental clinic booking flow running on WhatsApp, with dashboard events beside it](/images/blog/introducing-bigbrosai-flows/booking-chat.svg "A booking flow for a dental clinic: the patient picks a day and slot, gets tagged, and the front desk is notified automatically.")

## Building a flow, block by block

Click **Create flow**, give it a name and pick a starting point: a blank canvas, a welcome menu or lead capture. The flow builder opens with the block library on the left, grouped into messages, questions, logic, actions and AI.

Every flow begins with **Flow Start**, where you choose what triggers it:

- Keywords like *hi*, *appointment* or *book*
- A regex pattern for more flexible matching
- A template message button or a QR code campaign
- Any message, as a catch-all welcome

From there, drag in blocks and connect them. A **Text and Buttons** block greets the customer with up to three quick replies. **Ask Question** saves their answer into a variable. A **List Menu** offers more options, like open time slots. **Add Tag** labels the contact, **Notify Team** emails your staff, and a final message confirms everything.

:::tip
The **flow check** in the top bar tells you in real time if anything will break, like a button that isn't connected to anything. When it says *Flow looks good*, you're ready to test.
:::

## Or let AI build it for you

Don't want to drag blocks? Open **Build with AI** and describe your business in plain words. **Enhance** turns a short idea into a detailed brief, and in about a minute you get a complete, editable flow, with its trigger keywords ready.

![The Build with AI screen with a plain-English description of a dental clinic](/images/blog/introducing-bigbrosai-flows/build-with-ai.svg "Describe your business, press Generate, and edit the result like any other flow.")

Already have a flow? **Edit with AI** changes it from a single sentence, like *"add a step that asks for the patient's age"*. And **AI Routing** starts a flow even when customers don't type an exact keyword, based on a plain description of when it should run.

## AI answers from your own knowledge

The **AI Chatbot** block answers open questions from your **Knowledge Bank**: FAQs, text, PDFs or your website content. That means the bot answers with *your* timings, *your* prices and *your* policies, not generic internet answers.

And when it can't help, the **Human Handoff** block passes the conversation to a real person in your shared inbox, with everything the customer said so far.

## Test before you go live

Before switching a flow on for everyone, add up to three test numbers and turn on **Test mode**, so only you receive the flow. Then message your business on WhatsApp and watch it run, end to end. When you're happy, flip the toggle to take it live.
