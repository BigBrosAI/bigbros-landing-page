If you're running your business on the free WhatsApp Business app, you've probably felt its limits: one phone, a handful of devices, and a lot of copy-pasting. The **WhatsApp Business API** is Meta's platform for businesses that have outgrown the app. Here's what it actually changes, in plain words.

## 1. Your whole team on one number

With the API, everyone answers from the same business number in a **shared inbox**. Assign chats, leave internal notes and see who replied to what. No more passing one phone around the shop.

## 2. Broadcasts that reach your whole audience

The app's broadcast lists only reach people who have saved your number. On the API you can send campaigns to every customer who has **opted in**, personalised with their name and details, and scheduled in advance.

## 3. Chatbots that reply 24/7

Build a **chatbot flow** that greets customers, answers common questions, books appointments or qualifies leads at any hour, then hands over to a person when it matters. On BigBrosAI you can even [let AI build the flow](/blog/introducing-bigbrosai-flows) from a description of your business.

## 4. Interactive messages

Buttons, list menus, images, documents and product catalogues make it easy for customers to act with one tap, instead of typing a reply.

## 5. Automatic order and booking updates

Connect your store, sheet or system, and confirmations, reminders and shipping updates go out on their own. BigBrosAI integrates with [Shopify](/integrations/shopify), [WooCommerce](/integrations/woocommerce) and [Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets), plus a REST API and webhooks for anything else.

## 6. Payments inside the chat

Send a payment link, or let customers pay by UPI with **WhatsApp Pay**, without leaving the conversation. Fewer steps means fewer abandoned orders.

## 7. Ads that start conversations

**Click-to-WhatsApp ads** on Facebook and Instagram open a chat with your business. A flow can greet every lead instantly and tag them by the ad they came from.

## 8. Reports you can act on

See how many messages were delivered, read and clicked for every campaign, so you know what's working and what to change.

Your business profile shows your name, logo and details, and approved message templates keep communication consistent and professional. You can also apply for verification so customers know it's really you.

The API is built for volume. BigBrosAI handles up to 2,400 messages a minute on Basic and 5,000 on Pro, with queueing and retries built in, so a big launch doesn't break anything.
