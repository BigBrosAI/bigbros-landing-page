Patients already use WhatsApp every day. When your clinic messages them there, with the right message at the right time, they read it, they reply and they show up. Here are the eight messages we see working best for clinics, and how to set each one up.

![A grid of the eight WhatsApp messages for clinics, with their message categories](/images/blog/whatsapp-messages-for-clinics/message-map.svg "Most of these are utility messages, which cost far less than marketing messages.")

## 1. Appointment confirmation

Send it the moment a booking is made. Include the doctor, date, time and address, plus a button to add directions. It reassures the patient and stops the "is my appointment confirmed?" calls.

> Hi {{name}}, your appointment with Dr. {{doctor}} is confirmed for {{date}} at {{time}}. Reply MAP for directions.

## 2. Day-before reminder

This is the single biggest lever for reducing no-shows. Send it the day before with two buttons: **Confirm** and **Reschedule**. Patients who can't make it tell you in advance, and you can offer the slot to someone else.

## 3. Running-late notice

Doctors run late. A quick message when the queue is behind keeps patients from sitting in the waiting room for an hour and helps them trust your clinic more.

## 4. Report ready

Let patients know when lab results or scan reports are ready to collect or view. It cuts follow-up calls and gets patients back in for the next step sooner.

## 5. Follow-up check-in

A few days after a procedure or new prescription, ask how the patient is feeling. If they reply, the conversation goes straight to your team in the shared inbox. Patients remember the clinic that checked on them.

## 6. Recall reminder

Dental cleanings, eye check-ups, diabetes reviews and vaccinations all come around on a schedule. A recall reminder with a **Book now** button brings patients back on time, and fills your calendar on slow weeks.

## 7. Review request

After a good visit, ask for a Google review with a direct link. Happy patients are glad to help, they just need to be asked at the right moment.

## 8. Health tips and offers

Seasonal health tips, health camp announcements and check-up packages work well, **as long as patients have opted in** to hear from you. Keep these occasional and genuinely useful. These count as marketing messages.
