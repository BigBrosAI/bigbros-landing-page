For most Indian small businesses, WhatsApp is already where customers ask questions, negotiate and decide. The businesses growing fastest treat it as a sales channel, not just a chat app. You don't need a big budget to do the same. You need a simple system with four steps.

![The four steps of the WhatsApp customer journey: get found, reply instantly, close in chat and bring them back](/images/blog/grow-your-business-on-whatsapp/journey.svg)

## Step 1: Make it easy to find you on WhatsApp

Every place a customer sees your business should offer a one-tap way to chat.

- **QR codes** on your shop counter, menus, packaging, receipts and posters. Our [free WhatsApp QR code generator](/tools/whatsapp-qr-code-generator) makes one in seconds.
- **A chat button** on your website. Add one with the [free chat button generator](/tools/whatsapp-chat-button).
- **A wa.me link** in your Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and email signature, made with the [link generator](/tools/whatsapp-link-generator).
- **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** on Facebook and Instagram, which open a chat instead of a landing page.

:::tip
Add a pre-filled message to your links and QR codes, like *"Hi! I saw your poster and want to know about offers."* It tells you where the customer came from and makes the first message effortless.
:::

## Step 3: Close the sale inside the chat

Every time you send a customer to another website or app, some of them drop off. Keep them in the chat instead:

- Share products and prices directly in the conversation
- Collect details with WhatsApp Forms
- Send a payment link, or let them pay by UPI with WhatsApp Pay
- Confirm the order and send updates automatically

If you run an online store, [connecting Shopify](/integrations/shopify) or [WooCommerce](/integrations/woocommerce) sends order confirmations, COD confirmations and shipping updates on WhatsApp without anyone pressing send.

## Step 4: Bring customers back

Getting a new customer costs far more than keeping one. WhatsApp broadcasts let you reach customers who opted in with offers, launches and reminders, and they actually get read.

A few rules keep broadcasts effective:

1. Only message people who agreed to hear from you
2. Send relevant offers to the right segment, not everything to everyone
3. Keep it short with one clear button
4. Measure delivered, read and clicked, and improve each time
