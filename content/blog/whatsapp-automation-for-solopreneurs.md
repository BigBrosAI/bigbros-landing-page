When you run a business on your own, you're the marketer, the salesperson, the support team and the delivery person. Customer messages arrive while you're busy with all of that, and every slow reply is a sale at risk. Before you hire anyone, let WhatsApp automation take the repetitive work off your plate.

## The jobs automation can take over

Think about the messages you send most often. For most solopreneurs, they fall into a few buckets:

- **First replies:** "Hi! Thanks for reaching out, here's our price list."
- **Common questions:** availability, delivery time, payment options
- **Bookings:** scheduling calls, classes, fittings or appointments
- **Payments:** sending payment details and confirming receipts
- **Follow-ups:** reminders, thank-you notes, "how did you like it?"

Every one of these can run on its own.

## Step 1: An instant welcome

Set up a welcome flow that triggers on "hi" or any first message. Greet the customer by name, share what you offer and give three buttons for the most common next steps. It replies in seconds, even when you're asleep or mid-delivery.

## Step 2: Let the bot answer FAQs

Add your FAQs, price list or website to the **Knowledge Bank** and drop an **AI Chatbot** block into your flow. It answers questions in your words, and hands the chat to you when it can't.

:::tip
Write your FAQs exactly the way you'd answer a customer. The AI picks up your tone, so warm answers in means warm answers out.
:::

## Step 3: Bookings without back-and-forth

If you sell your time, a booking flow saves hours. Offer available days and slots as buttons, save the customer's choice and get notified by email. No more "Is 4 PM okay? How about 5?"

## Step 4: Get paid in the chat

Send a **payment link** or use **WhatsApp Pay** so customers can pay by UPI without leaving the conversation. Payments land in your account, and you can see every transaction in one place.
