Search for "bulk WhatsApp sender" and you'll find dozens of tools promising to message thousands of people from your personal number. Most of them break WhatsApp's terms, and the usual result is a banned number and a damaged reputation. There's a better way that's fully allowed, more reliable and often cheaper than you'd expect.

![Comparison of the official WhatsApp Business API and unofficial bulk sending tools](/images/blog/send-bulk-whatsapp-messages-officially/official-vs-unofficial.svg)

## Why unofficial bulk senders are risky

Unofficial tools usually automate a regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account. WhatsApp actively detects this. Common outcomes:

- Your number gets **temporarily or permanently banned**, sometimes after a single campaign
- There's **no record of opt-in**, so recipients report you as spam
- You get **no reliable delivery reports**, so you can't tell what worked
- Customers see messages from an unknown number, which hurts trust

If your business depends on WhatsApp, losing your number is a real cost.

## The official route: the WhatsApp Business API

The WhatsApp Business API is Meta's approved way to message customers at scale. You connect a number through a provider like BigBrosAI, and send **broadcasts** using **approved message templates** to people who have **opted in**. Done properly, it's fast, measurable and safe.

## Step 1: Get opt-in

Only message people who agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. Collect opt-in at checkout, on forms, at your shop counter with a QR code, or when customers first message you. Keep a note of how each contact opted in.

## Step 2: Clean and import your list

Make sure numbers include country codes and remove duplicates. Our free [contact list cleaner](/tools/whatsapp-number-formatter) does this in seconds. Then upload your contacts to BigBrosAI and **tag** them by interest, location or purchase history.

## Step 3: Create an approved template

Bulk messages that start a conversation must use a template approved by Meta. Write it clearly, say who you are, use variables like the customer's name, and add a button. Check it first with the free [template builder](/tools/whatsapp-template-builder), which flags common rejection reasons.

:::tip
Add a **Stop updates** quick-reply button to marketing broadcasts. It makes opting out easy, which protects your number's quality rating.
:::

## Step 4: Send to a segment, not everyone

A relevant message to 2,000 interested customers usually beats a generic one to 20,000. Pick a tagged segment, schedule the send for a time your audience is active, and launch.
